A reunion of sorts

Ten years since our first Costa Rica retreat

In the spring of 2016 we led a retreat together on Costa Rica's Pacific coast. We surfed badly and then a little better, practiced twice a day, and laughed together into the evening with a group who became friends.

A decade later we have each built something of our own. Patricia founded Love Surf Yoga and has led more than sixty retreats around the world. Jacob built Embodied Philosophy into a school for the study and practice of the contemplative traditions. This March we are bringing both back to the place it started.

If you were with us then, come home. If you are new, you are exactly who we hoped would come.

Patricia & Jacob

Your hosts

Two friends and founders, back on the playa

Patricia Pinto

Founder, Love Surf Yoga

Patricia has led more than sixty retreats and teacher trainings across the world, from Mexico and Colombia to Yosemite. Her classes blend yoga, Pilates-inspired movement, meditation, yoga nidra and sound, and she has a gift for making first-time surfers and first-time yogis feel at home on the same mat.

Love Surf Yoga

Jacob Kyle

Founding Director, Embodied Philosophy

Jacob is a scholar-practitioner of Kashmir Śaiva philosophy and a doctoral candidate at the University of Oxford. Since 2015 he has built Embodied Philosophy, home to Sādhana School, Tarka Journal and the CHITHEADS podcast. His teaching brings the texts of the yoga traditions into conversation with daily practice, and he trained in the contemplative lineage of Paul Muller-Ortega.

Questions

Good to know

I've never surfed. Is this for me?

Yes. Santa Teresa has beginner-friendly waves and lessons are tailored to your level. Plenty of guests come for the yoga and philosophy and spend surf time in a hammock.

Do I need to know anything about philosophy?

No. Jacob's sessions are conversational and open to everyone. Pre-retreat reading is there if you want it.

How do I get there?

Fly into Liberia (LIR) or San José (SJO). From Liberia it is about a four-hour drive; small planes also fly to Tambor, around 40 minutes from Santa Teresa. We'll help coordinate shared transfers.

Can I come alone?

Many guests do. Choose a shared room and we'll pair you with a roommate, or book a private room.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, a light layer for evenings, and a journal. Yoga mats and props are provided at the shala.
